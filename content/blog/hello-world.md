## How to write a post

Create a new `.md` file in `content/blog/`. The file name becomes the URL, so
`thematic-analysis-notes.md` is published at `/blog/thematic-analysis-notes/`.

Each post starts with a short header:

- `title` and `description` are required.
- `pubDate` sets the order on the blog page.
- `tags` is optional.
- `draft: true` hides the post from the published site.

## Ideas for early posts

- A method reflection: what you learned running your last set of interviews.
- A short reading note on a paper that shaped your framing.
- A conference recap with three things you took away.
